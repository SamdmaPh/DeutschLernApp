The Problem

Millions are "learning" a language — with nothing to show for it.

  • Gamified apps optimise for streaks and addiction, not fluency. People keep 1,000-day streaks and still can't order a coffee in German.
  • Endless tapping of colourful bubbles ≠ real conversation skills.
  • Grammar is taught like a textbook — dry, abstract, forgettable.
  • Learners quit because they never feel the payoff of actually speaking.

The result: high engagement metrics, low real-world competence.

The Solution

A German app built around speaking from day one.

  • Real sentences. Real conversations. Real progress.
  • Every lesson built around phrases you'll use immediately.
  • Grammar explained like a human, not a textbook.
  • Science-backed: flashcards, spaced repetition, quizzes & mnemonics in one smooth flow that sticks.
  • A love letter to the language itself — its beauty, its untranslatable words, its soul.

How a correction works

Your sentence: "Ich habe gegangen."
↓
Corrected: "Ich bin gegangen."
↓
Why: movement verbs take sein, not haben.
